## About

The Margin of Error is an independent publication by
[Hans W. A. Hanley](https://www.hanshanley.com/) featuring essays on computer
science, disinformation, statistics, politics, and world events.

The archive brings together work first published on Substack, reporting
published with the Atlantic Council's Digital Forensic Research Lab, earlier
essays, and new writing.
