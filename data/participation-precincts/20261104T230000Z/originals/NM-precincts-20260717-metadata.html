Description
Abstract The data are intended for use as a general reference to the extent and boundaries of the 2021 New Mexico Precincts. The 2021 precinct boundaries were updated as part of the New Mexico Secretary of State's election boundary project. This July 2026 release has the update of the Sandoval Precincts which includes 165 Districts.
Purpose The data are intended for use as a general reference to the extent and boundaries of the New Mexico Voting Districts/Precincts.
