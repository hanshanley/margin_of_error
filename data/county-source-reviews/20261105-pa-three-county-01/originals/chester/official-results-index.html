NOTICE

On Tuesday, November 3rd at 7:00am, The County Board of Elections will conduct the central count of ballots which will take place on the 5th floor, located at 313 W. Market St., West Chester, PA.

The Chester County Board of Elections will conduct rolling legal review as ballots are processed during the 24/7 central scan operation starting on November 3rd at 7:00am which potentially will continue the days thereafter, located on the 5th floor, located at 313 W. Market St., West Chester, PA.

The official computation of the votes cast & the provisional votes cast at the General Election held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, in Chester County, as returned by the election officers of the precincts, will begin at 9:00AM on Friday, November 6, 2026, on the 5th floor, located at 313 W. Market St., West Chester, PA.
