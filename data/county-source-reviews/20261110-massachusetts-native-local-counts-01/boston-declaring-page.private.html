Unofficial Election Results

We have unofficial results for the State Election held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Between 8 and 9 p.m. on Election Night, the Election Department will publish results from mail-in and in-person early voting ballots processed in advance at Boston City Hall by 5 p.m. on Friday, October 30, 2026. These are referred to as Central Tabulation results. All mail-in and in-person early voting ballots that were not processed in advance at City Hall were sent to polling locations to be processed on Election Day.

All ballots must reach the Election Department or a ballot box by 8 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, or be postmarked by Tuesday, November 3 and arrive by Friday, November 6, 2026.

The unofficial results available on the website on Election Night will also be available by precinct on Wednesday, November 4, 2026. The deadline to certify official results is Wednesday, November 18, 2026 . Learn more about how Election results are reported .
