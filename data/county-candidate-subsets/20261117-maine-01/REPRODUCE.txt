No requests, respondent fitting or external data are required.

The inputs include both complete retained native district responses, their
receipts, complete selected Census county geometry facts and the versioned
candidate/source-frame review. Geometry facts were extracted from the retained
official TIGER/Line 2026 county archive by the existing county compiler, with
no repairs. This command reproduces results from those complete selected facts;
it does not claim to rerun national TIGER extraction. The full original national
source is retained in the preceding immutable complete county-results download:
https://github.com/hanshanley/margin_of_error/releases/download/voter-county-2026-20261110t071244z/complete-county-results.zip

These are retained 2026-11-07 round-1 candidate counts, not latest or final RCV
results, ballots, unique participants or turnout. Democratic and Republican
county vectors reconcile exactly. All 165 unlocated write-ins remain in the
district decisions. An affected county's all-candidate margin bounds allow
0..165 additional write-ins there; bounds are marginal, not simultaneous
allocations or confidence intervals. No swing, model or participation
eligibility is granted. No write-ins are allocated or silently set to zero.
